Support

Questions, bugs or deletion requests: brooksscole@gmail.com. I usually reply within a couple of days.

Where does the data come from?

Public sources only: SEC EDGAR filings (including structured XBRL data), company press releases and ETF issuer closure notices. The app downloads a public data file that is regenerated on a schedule, and every figure links to its source. Figures that can’t be confirmed are shown as Unknown, never guessed.

Is this investment advice?

No. Reforged is an information and research tool. Data may be incomplete or out of date, and post-bankruptcy stocks are highly risky. Do your own research.

How do I report a comment?

Long-press or swipe on the comment and choose Report. You can also block the person who posted it, which hides their comments right away. Reports are reviewed by the developer, and objectionable content is removed. You can also email the address above.

How do I delete my comments?

Swipe or long-press on your own comment and choose Delete. Private notes live only on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app. To have a public comment removed for you, email the address above.
