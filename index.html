Reforged

A quiet tracker for companies relisting after Chapter 11, and the industry ETFs that close as sectors consolidate.

  • Ranked relisting candidates with owners, resale overhangs, lockups and sources
  • Scores against your own editable “ideal candidate” template
  • ETF closure notices with dates and source links
  • Reminders and widgets before key dates

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything cites public filings.

Reforged is a research tool, not investment advice.
