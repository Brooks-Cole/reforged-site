Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Reforged ("the app") is a research and note-taking app for following ETF closures and post-bankruptcy stock relistings. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: the app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Almost everything stays on your iPhone.

Data stored only on your device

The following stays on your iPhone (and in your device backups). The developer never receives it:

  • Private comments and notes
  • Your settings (notification preferences, display name, custom feed URL)
  • Your edits to the ideal-candidate template, hidden items, and which items you've already viewed
  • A cached copy of the research data
  • Moderation choices: comments you've reported and users you've blocked

Widgets, Spotlight search results and reminders are produced on your device from this data. Reminders are local notifications; the app doesn't use push notifications.

Public comments (optional, iCloud)

If public comments are turned on in your copy of the app and you post one, the comment is stored in the app's public iCloud (CloudKit) database, operated by Apple. It contains:

  • the comment text, the item it's about, and when it was posted
  • the display name you chose (optional; it can be left blank)
  • an anonymous, app-specific iCloud identifier that Apple attaches to the record. It isn't your Apple ID or email address.

Anyone using the app can read public comments. You can delete your own public comments in the app at any time. If you report a comment, a report record (the reason, the reported comment's text and identifiers, and the time) is stored in the same database so the developer can review it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Public comments must follow the in-app community terms. Objectionable content isn't tolerated and is removed, and users who post it are removed.

Data downloaded by the app

To stay current, the app downloads a public data file (the "feed") from a web host such as GitHub, or from a custom address you enter. The app sends no personal data in that request. Like any website, the host may log technical details such as your IP address. Tapping a source link opens that website in your browser, under that site's own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising or third-party tracking
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No location, contacts, photos, health, financial-account or payment data
  • No tracking across apps or websites (as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency)

Children

The app isn't directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete private data by deleting the app.
  • Delete your public comments in the app, or email the developer to request deletion.
  • Turn off iCloud sharing of public comments in Settings > Comments.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will change too. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
